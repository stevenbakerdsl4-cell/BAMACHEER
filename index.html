Competitive Teams

We compete
as Thunder.

Every BCX athlete who makes a team wears the Thunder name onto the floor. It's earned at evaluations, built in practice, and proven on the score sheet — a full competitive season of choreography, conditioning, and travel to regional and national events.

  • SeasonPlacements run through evaluations each year, with teams formed by age and skill level.
  • PracticeStructured weekly practice blocks focused on stunt technique, tumbling progressions, and full-routine run-throughs.
  • CompetingTeams travel to regional competitions throughout the season, working toward national-level events.
  • CultureParents describe it as a second family — practices, competitions, and everything in between.

Meet the Coach

AnQuetta Diggins Shealey

A University of Alabama graduate who built a gym in her own backyard of West Alabama — and has been running it ever since.

AnQuetta didn't set out to start a business first. She set out to give kids in Northport and Tuscaloosa a place to learn cheer the right way — technique first, confidence built in right alongside it. That's still the standard every coach on staff is held to at Bama Cheer Xtreme today.

Ask any parent what makes the gym different and technique rarely comes up first. They talk about how their kid is treated — pushed hard, looked after harder, and known by name from their very first tiny class.

A. ShealeyOwner & Head Coach, Bama Cheer Xtreme
